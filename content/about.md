## Hey, I'm David 👋

I'm a software engineer with 5+ years building and operating production software across IoT,
cloud, and real-time data platforms. Most of my work sits at the seam between hardware and the
cloud — the layer where physical devices become something you can query, monitor, and trust.

Go is my primary backend language, running in production across a logistics platform and internal
engineering tooling, with TypeScript in day-to-day use on the front end.

## What I Do

I work hands-on across the stack: a hand-rolled serial protocol talking to USB-connected devices
at one end, AWS infrastructure and APIs at the other, and firmware validation and device telemetry
in between. I collaborate closely with hardware teams, which has taught me that the interesting
bugs usually live in the gap between two systems that each work fine alone.

I care about small, well-bounded components — code a teammate can pick up cold and change without
fear.

## Experience

Since 2024 I've been at **Wynd**, building internal tools and backend services for an IoT sensor
platform — Go tooling that supports engineering and operations workflows. Before that I contracted
with **Woven by Toyota** on structured real-world data collection for AI/ML production pipelines,
and spent two years self-employed, building a web management platform for a last-mile logistics
company that streamlined internal processes and cut logistics costs by roughly 10%.

## Things I've Built

**[ChillCheck](https://github.com/davidbuenonnoleto/chillcheck)** — cold-chain temperature
compliance for restaurants. Replaces paper temperature logs with inspection-ready PDF reports.
A Go API (chi, pgx, JWT) with an alert engine and Stripe billing, a React/TypeScript frontend,
and a standalone Go Bluetooth gateway agent that runs on a Raspberry Pi and forwards sensor
readings automatically.
